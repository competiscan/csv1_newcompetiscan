Turn your junk mail into rewards!


You’re invited to participate in a market research study of direct mail and email marketing materials received by consumers or business owners. In exchange for sending us your mail and email solicitations, we will compensate you for those items you might otherwise throw away.

If you are a business owner or self-employed, you will earn bonus points for the materials you send, accelerating the time it takes you to earn rewards!

Becoming a Consumer Panelist or Business Owner Panelist


As a Consumer Panelist or Business Owner Panelist,* you’ll be asked to regularly send us direct mail and email communications related to the following:

Insurance: Health, Life, Home, Auto, Travel Protection, Prescription Drug, etc.
Investments: Annuities, IRAs, Mutual Funds, Brokerage, 401(k), Pensions, etc.
Loans: Mortgages, Home Equity, Auto loans, Personal Loan, etc.


Banking: Checking, Savings, Money Market, CDs, Rewards or Loyalty Programs, Banking Services, Business Banking, Credit Unions etc.


Credit Cards: All Types (General, Business, Merchant, Secured), Balance transfer offers, Credit Score Providers, Debt Settlement, etc.
Travel: Airlines, Hotels/Accommodations, Cruise Lines, Car Rental Services
Telecom: Wireless (Mobile), Cable and Fiber, Satellite
Auto: Auto Manufacturers, Dealerships, Auto Parts Stores and Auto Suppliers
Retail: Clothing, Cosmetics, Home Improvement, Pet Supplies, Pharmacy, Shipping, Supercenters, etc. No Catalogs, Sale Flyers or Coupons Please!
Energy: Account Management, Billing, Green Programs, Service Alerts, Usage Options, Tips
Real Estate: Any Real Estate (Transactional, Educational or Promotional), Property Management
Other: Consumer Services, HR/Payroll/HCM/PEO, Pharmaceuticals, Non-Profit, Shipping, Technology, etc.

Unsure? Send it anyway! As long as it isn’t on the “Do Not Send” list, we want to see it!

How You’ll Be Compensated

By becoming a Consumer Panelist or Business Owner Panelist, you will earn participation points that can be redeemed for a MasterCard/Visa prepaid card.


To earn points, simply do the following:



 Direct Mail: Place your qualifying direct mail in the postage-paid envelopes we provide and return them to us about once a week.
Email: Forward us your qualifying emails.
Digital Ad Tracking: Download our Chrome Extension for extra points every month.


Bonuses: Earn extra points or direct "prepaid" cards for participating in periodic bonus opportunities.

"Frequent participants can earn as much as $20 in points every 6 to 10 weeks! Business owners and those who are self-employed can earn rewards even faster."

To start earning points for valuable rewards, we ask that you complete a brief form with some basic information about yourself to complete your confidential Panelist Profile. Rest assured that all personal information is eliminated and you will never be solicited as a result of your participation. There is no commitment and you may opt out at any time.
