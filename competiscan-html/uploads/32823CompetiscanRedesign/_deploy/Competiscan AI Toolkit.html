What you get

Every run produces polished, presentation-ready documents, generated with your logo and brand colors applied automatically.

DME Report

Your creative execution, scored and explained.

A full written analysis of every asset you upload. For each creative, the report includes:

  • Category scores on a 1–10 scale across 10 weighted dimensions, from visual design quality and storytelling to call-to-action clarity, ease of response, and audience fit.
  • Channel-aware weighting: an email is not judged like a mailer. Subject lines matter for email, the first 5 seconds matter for video, response friction matters for direct mail.
  • Detailed critiques explaining every score: what's working, what's holding the piece back, and how it compares to benchmark entries from the Competiscan archive.

DME Presentation

Your results, ready to present.

Skip the deck-building. Compass turns the DME analysis into a branded PowerPoint automatically:

  • One slide per creative with the campaign visual, title, and its performance tier (High / Average / Low performing).
  • A “Category Scores by Campaign” comparison slide that puts all your entries side by side.
  • Generated with your logo and brand colors, ready to drop into your next stakeholder meeting.

CRE Report

Your offer vs. the real market.

A competitive positioning analysis that compares your product offer against a universe of real competitor products drawn from Competiscan's proprietary database, filtered to your sector and audience. Inside:

  • An overall competitiveness score with section-by-section breakdowns.
  • Pricing competitiveness: exactly where your pricing wins and where it loses.
  • Rewards program positioning against live competitor offers.
  • Strategic insights and differentiators you can take straight into product and marketing decisions.

One upload. Instant reports. Zero manual work.
